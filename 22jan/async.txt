->setTimeout method is like java's sleep method , it takes two arguments one is the callback function and the other one is the timeOut time in ms, it executes Asynchronously
->non-blocking Asynchronous / blocking Asynchronous / blocking synchronous / non blocking synchronous -> every instruction is done by the js engine
